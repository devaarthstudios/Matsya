MATSYA

DIVE · GLOW · DEEPER

A calm, glowing dive through sunken river ghats, temples and crystal caves. Drag your finger to steer a little luminous fish through ornamental gates, pillars and turning wheels. Free to play, no ads, works completely offline.

WHAT IS IN THE GAME

One finger

Drag to steer. Nothing else to learn. The dive speeds up the deeper you go.

Eight painted worlds

From Ganga Ghat to Dwarka, Surya Mandir and the Ratna Guha crystal cave. The scene changes every 140 metres.

Five obstacle styles

Gates, breath pockets, pillars, turning wheels and zig-zag walls, each with its own ornamental pattern.

Power-ups

Chakra Dash lets you phase through everything at super speed. Shield, Magnet, Slow and Wide help too, each with a timer ring.

Original music

A sitar and tabla score made for the game, with separate buttons to mute music, pearl sounds and effects.

Graphics setting

Choose low, medium or high graphics from the menu. The game also lowers the setting by itself when it detects slow frames.

How to play in four lines

  1. Drag anywhere to steer the fish. Dive as deep as you can.
  2. Slip through the gaps. Touching an obstacle ends the dive.
  3. Collect pearls, golden lotus pearls and glowing power-up orbs.
  4. Watch the timer rings on the left to see which power-ups are active.
