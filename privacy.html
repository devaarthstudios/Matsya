Privacy Policy

Matsya by Devaarth Studios. Last updated: October 16, 2026.

Matsya is a mobile game made by Devaarth Studios. This page explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: the game does not collect anything, and it cannot send anything anywhere.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, request, or receive any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign in, no email address required, and no contact list access.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves a few things locally on your phone so it can remember your progress between sessions. This information never leaves your device:

  • Your deepest dive and your total pearls collected
  • Your music, pearl sound and effects on or off settings
  • Your graphics quality setting

This data lives in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. If you uninstall Matsya, this data is deleted with it.

3. Internet access

Matsya does not ask for the Internet permission. Because of that, the app is technically unable to send any information off your device, even if it wanted to. The game works fully offline.

4. Permissions the app uses

Matsya does not request any Android permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files, and it does not read your advertising ID.

5. Advertising and analytics

There are no ads in Matsya. There are no analytics tools, no crash reporting services, and no third party tracking software. Nothing is profiled and nothing is sold.

6. Children's privacy

Matsya is not directed at children under 13. Since the game collects no information at all, it also does not knowingly collect anything from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided information to us, contact us and we will help.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. Any data the game creates stays on your device and disappears when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the new version of this policy.
